# 0012. SLO values

Status: accepted 2026-11-10 (user chose the "Small SMB demo" scale at intake)
Context: ≤ 50 staff users and ≤ 500 employees per company; 20 req/s peak.
Options:
1. **Small SMB demo:** 99.5%, p95 300 ms, ~20 req/s. Chosen.
2. **Growing SaaS:** 99.9%, p95 200 ms, ~200 req/s. Heavier load tests and more replicas.
3. **Minimal:** 99%, p95 500 ms, ~5 req/s. Smoke tests only.
Decision (option 1):
- BFF availability 99.5% monthly (error budget 0.5%).
- p95 < 300 ms for reads and simple writes.
- p95 < 1.5 s for calculate and approve on a 500-employee run.
- Payout settlement p95 ≤ 60 s for a 500-employee run, on the **healthy-bank path**. During a bank outage, attempts stay `indeterminate` and are re-driven until the bank answers (ADR 0003); `PayoutSettlementSlow` and `PayoutsStuck` cover that case.
- Load tests: smoke 5 VUs × 30 s (PR); full 30 req/s × 10 min plus a soak at 5 req/s × 30 min (nightly).
- Burn-rate alerts: page at 14.4× over 1 h and 5 m; ticket at 6× over 6 h and 30 m, against a budget of 0.005.
Consequences: revisit at the "Growing SaaS" tier (99.9%, 200 req/s).
